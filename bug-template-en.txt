{panel:title=🐞 Bug summary|bgColor=#FEEBE7}
One sentence: what's broken and where. _E.g.: "clicking 'Publish' does nothing"._
{panel}

h3. 🔥 Impact & priority
Who is affected and how, how urgent. _Don't forget to set the Priority field._

h3. ⏱️ Time estimate
*What we budget:* _e.g. 4h / 2d_ — _Don't forget the Original estimate field._

h3. 📋 Description & steps to reproduce
# Step 1…
# Step 2…

*Expected:* …
*Actual:* …
_Attach screenshots / gifs inline._

h3. 🔍 Root cause
Why it happened — filled by BA / Dev during triage.

h3. 🔌 For clients (iOS / Android / Web)
_Server response does not change after the fix — say so in one line._
* *Endpoints:* which ones are affected — link to the operation in Swagger or to https://spec.matrix.org/latest/
* *What changed:* fields, codes (HTTP + errcode), behaviour
* *Client change needed:* yes / no, what exactly

h3. ✅ Test cases
# Clear step-by-step scenario to verify the fix.

h3. 🔗 Links & notes
Links to tickets, threads, feedback, follow-up tasks. Technical details: DB queries, logs, etc.
